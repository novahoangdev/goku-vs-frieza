# v10: aerial perspective

Scene
- Ground, sea, rock pillars and tree canopies fade toward the turquoise of the horizon with distance (from 25 m, up to 38% at 180 m):
  distant rock pillars recede clearly, characters and the foreground read with more volume. Before this a pillar 150 m away was as dark and sharp
  as the one right behind Goku, so the frame looked flat.

Fixes
- At the start of the chase (frames ~810–830) Goku, Frieza and the camera flew straight through the core of the first rock pillar: the pillar sat right
  on the flight path, so the frame only showed rock texture all around, and in some frames the camera ended up inside Goku's body (an orange screen).
  Moved that pillar aside, ~1.2 m from the flight path (skimming past like the last pillar).

App
- Anime-style Death Beam: the beam is thin and pointed at the fingertip, then widens; a 4-point star flash + crackling spikes at the fingertip, a white
  light streak running along the beam, zigzag sparks shooting out from both edges (keeps the 3 bands purple → pink → white).
