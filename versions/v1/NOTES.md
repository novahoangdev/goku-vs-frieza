# v1: first chibi (18:15, 29/10)

- Chibi characters built from basic shapes: sphere head, eyes made of stacked flat shapes (white, iris, pupil, highlight).
- Goku's hair: a cluster of curved spikes + a tuft; the SSJ version is bright yellow.
- Frieza: purple head dome is a half sphere, red eyes, tail in 8 segments.
- Namek scene: rock pillars, round trees, sea, dark clouds, black cracks spreading around Goku.
- Obvious limitations: the face sits too high, leaving the chin area empty; shot 7b has a stretch where nobody is visible; the Frieza chase shot hides Goku; at the end Goku is cut off at the frame edge.
