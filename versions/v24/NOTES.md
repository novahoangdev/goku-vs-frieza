# v24: black-haired Goku in the DBZ silhouette

Before v24 the black hair was a bundle of thin spikes radiating evenly around the head like a hedgehog, and the bangs were a black rim capping the whole forehead → it didn't read as Goku's head.
`goku_hair_forms` (black hair) rebuilt from DBZ Goku reference images:

- A top cluster of 3 long layered locks all going the same way: shooting up then sweeping sideways to the character's right; a secondary top lock and 2 low back-crown
  locks leaning to both sides → seen head-on the whole hair mass flows to one side as in the reference.
- 4 long layered locks pointing sideways on each side: upturned on the character's right, tips drooping slightly on the left (asymmetric like the original).
- Locks with a thick base tapering long to a sharp tip (`tri` tapering by `(1-s)^0.95`) instead of thin needle spikes.
- Fanning out at the back of the head, jagged edge at the nape.
- Bangs: thick locks falling over the forehead to the brows (the character's right lock curving out to the tail of the brow, a long middle lock hanging between the brows, a left lock
  and a long lock hugging the left cheek down to eye level), 2 temple locks, pointed sideburns in front of the ears. Mid-forehead hairline el 30 → 40.
- Slightly lighter black tone (#17171F → #22232E) so the bang locks separate instead of merging into one black mass.

Only the frames where the black hair still shows (0–450 after retiming) were re-rendered; the rest is kept from v23.
