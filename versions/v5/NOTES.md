# v5: finishing blow lands + polished climax

Choreography
- Goku appears right behind Frieza and lunges, the finishing punch lands squarely on Frieza's face (in v1–v4 Goku stood 1.2 m away
  and punched the air).

Super Saiyan transformation
- Building emotion: normal eyes → angry lowered lids (236–292) → "> <" squeezed shut while screaming → determined teal eyes.
- Three SSJ flashes before the transformation: golden hair at nearly full size (in v4 the hair was small and showed a bald head).
- Removed the center bang spike of the black hair (in eye close-ups a black hair tip poked between the eyes).

Effects
- Frieza's fingertip gathers pink Ki (swells then bursts) before each Death Beam and ki blast; it is on the energy layer, so
  the app adds a glow.

Model
- Frieza's tail is a single seamless NURBS curve, tapering, hooked to the 8 tail joints so it still bends with the choreography
  (in v1–v4 it was 8 capsule segments joined together, with visible notches).
- Goku's fist has 4 knuckles on the punching face plus a thumb (clearly visible in the crash zoom on the finishing blow).
- Frieza waiting in the sky and Goku at the end bob gently as if hovering (before, they stood still like statues).

Camera angles
- Shot 6b over Frieza's shoulder: camera tight behind his head, 62–68 mm telephoto lens; Frieza's head dome + shoulder pad frame the right edge, Goku
  large and clear in the middle of the frame (in v4 Goku was tiny in the middle of an empty field and Frieza's shoulder fell out of frame).

Chibi-style expressions
- Frieza gets a 💢 anger vein on his forehead when he sees Goku transform, and a sweat drop when he loses track of Goku.
- SSJ Goku smirks confidently after the transformation and after dodging all the Death Beams.
- Frieza laughs a mocking open-mouthed "ho ho ho" right before he sees Goku transform.
- SSJ hair blown back by the wind while flying in the chase.

App
- New app icon drawn with Codex (chibi SSJ Goku on an orange-yellow background), also used for the splash screen (previously Expo's default "A" icon).
- The charge-Ki screen poster shows as soon as the app opens (a React Native layer over the Canvas, shaking with the charge strength); in v4 the poster only appeared after
  the video finished decoding, so it was still black for ~2 seconds.
- Anime-style ki blast explosion: white flash, 3-tone fireball with a spiky rim, shockwave ring, the fire collapses inward then purple-gray
  smoke gradually takes over; sits behind Goku (in v4 it was a flat orange-red blob over Goku's body).
